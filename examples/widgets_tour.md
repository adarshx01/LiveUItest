# Test: Widget interactions

URL: http://127.0.0.1:8800/widgets/modal

## Steps
1. Click Open settings
2. Enter Display name as QA Bot
3. Click Save
4. Click Yes, save
5. Verify that "Settings saved for QA Bot" is displayed
6. Click Delete draft
7. Dismiss the confirmation dialog
8. Verify that "Deletion cancelled" is displayed
9. Go to http://127.0.0.1:8800/widgets/tabs
10. Click Terms of Service
11. Switch to the new tab
12. Verify that "Terms of Service" is displayed
13. Switch back to the original tab
14. Check the I have read the terms checkbox
15. Verify the I have read the terms checkbox is checked
